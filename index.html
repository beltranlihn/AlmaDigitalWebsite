The Team

03

Founder & Creative Director

Vicente Manzano

Vicente Manzano is a multidisciplinary artist with a background in film direction and production. He earned his BFA in Filmmaking from NYFA in Los Angeles, California, and has developed in the fields of music production and digital art. His innovative approach, holistic vision, and passion for immersive storytelling led him to found Alma Digital, where he continues to explore and redefine the intersection of art and technology.

Co-founder & Creative Technologist Director

Beltran Lihn

With a background as an architect and a Master's degree from the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile, he brings a unique perspective to his projects, blending his skills as a music producer and 3D artist. His work is driven by a passion for interdisciplinary collaboration, sound design, and the integration of diverse tools to visualize compelling narratives.
