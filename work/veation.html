Overview

VEATION is immersive video editing software by Alma Digital Studio. Fulldome, 360 rooms and flat screens share one timeline, and the work is edited in its final format, in real time.

Concept

Every dome show we made started the same way: cut it flat, render it, walk into the dome and find out. So we built the editor we wanted. The dome view plays while you edit, with 26 GPU effects, audio-reactive visuals, looping motion without keyframes and an export that comes out exactly as you saw it.

Rito Digital, the studio's fulldome concert, was made in VEATION and performed live in the CULTVR dome in Cardiff. VEATION itself was presented and demonstrated in that dome at FulldomeUK 2026, and is now in private beta with studios, artists and venues in eight countries.
