Fashion Show Micsur

Immersive Design — Live Performance

Overview

Developed for MICSUR and presented at the Museo de Arte Contemporáneo, this project integrated immersive visual environments with a live fashion show, combining original music performed in real-time with an immersive sound system mixed in Dolby Atmos.
