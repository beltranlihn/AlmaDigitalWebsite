Soul Charger is a 15-minute VR experience merging neuroaesthetics, biofeedback, and applied bioethics, by Alma Digital with Johns Hopkins Berman Institute of Bioethics IDEA's Lab.
Five stages—Entering (Breath), Recognizing (Heart), Loving (Mind), Attracting (Touch), Surrounding (Movement)—each target specific brainwave states through responsive environments driven by real-time EEG and HRV data.
Central is the "Soul Amoeba," a translucent entity evolving from dormant to radiant as users "charge" it, transforming abstract biometrics into emotional engagement. The experience concludes with a personalized data sculpture—a new model for ethically-grounded immersive wellness art.

Collaboration

A year-long partnership with Johns Hopkins University

Berman Institute of Bioethics · iDeas Lab

Following Alma Digital's Pitch XR Award at Mediamorfosis Fest, a formal collaboration began — nearly a year of monthly mentorship sessions. In March 2025, Vicente Manzano visited Washington D.C. for in-person sessions and co-led a public conversation at the Inter-American Development Bank, where Neurocosm was exhibited — the biofeedback foundation upon which Soul Charger was built.

Looking ahead

  • Formal pilot study with pre/post assessments and potential co-authored publications on ethical frameworks for biofeedback art
  • Deepened collaboration with the Arts and Mind Lab on neuroaesthetics
  • Academic and artistic launch event at Barcelona XRLab or Johns Hopkins

Four key developments

01

From guided meditation to conscious introspective game

User choices, reflective exercises, and biometric responses co-author the experience in real time — moving beyond instructional voice-over.

02

Privacy as design: a new model for data consent

Biometric data made visually active throughout. Users who share their data sculpture can view others', turning transparency into a communal act.

03

Bioethics as lived experience

Ethical questions arise organically from the visceral encounter with real-time biometric data — not positioned as a compliance layer.

04

Scientific research potential & neuroaesthetics

Research hypothesis established across three core questions. Opened a new frontier with the Arts and Mind Lab (IAM Lab) at Johns Hopkins.
