Concepto

Neural Canvas VR is an interactive VR painting experience created by Alma Digital Studio for the EIT Culture & Creativity Innovation Forum.

Each participant steps into a virtual studio and paints freely for two minutes while an EEG sensor tracks their state of calm, folding brain activity into the creative gesture itself.

The 200 individual sessions are assembled into a single collective artwork, unveiled live at the EUIPO headquarters in Alicante. The piece distills the studio's thesis: creativity, biofeedback and wellbeing in one measurable, shared experience.
