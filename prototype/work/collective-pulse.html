Concepto

Real-time biometric installation for groups. Visitors place a finger on a pulse sensor and their heartbeat appears on a large-scale projection as a living organic form, beating at their own rhythm.

When several people are read at the same time, their forms share the same field: they drift toward one another or pull apart. The piece runs on a custom real-time engine developed in-house, rendering at 60fps. Nothing is pre-rendered — every form exists only during the session that produced it, and disappears with it.

The work makes no claim that an audience synchronises. What the room shows is behaviour, attraction and dispersion, and what it asks is older than the sensor: what, if anything, a group of bodies shares when they are read together.
